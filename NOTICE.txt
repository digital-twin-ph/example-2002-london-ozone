Ozone and deaths in London, 2002-2006
=====================================

Data:     londondataset2002_2006.dta, as published by the authors with
          Bhaskaran K, Gasparrini A, Hajat S, Smeeth L, Armstrong B. Time
          series regression studies in environmental epidemiology. Int J
          Epidemiol 2013;42(4):1187-1195. doi:10.1093/ije/dyt092
          Copied from github.com/gasparrini/2013_bhaskaran_IJE_codedata at
          commit 364752c07e37ab41e14d827480dff106f035ec26, unchanged.

Basis:    The paper states no terms for the data and the repository carries
          no licence. The authors write that "Stata and R code to reproduce
          our analyses, along with the dataset itself, are available as a
          Supplementary Appendix", and the repository's README invites readers
          to "Download as a ZIP file using the green button Clone or download
          above". This example reproduces their analyses, for
          non-commercial teaching, with citation. That is our reading of their
          intent, not a licence they have stated. The article is CC BY-NC 3.0.

Changes:  None.

The authors are not associated with this example and have not endorsed it.
